ALPA IMAGE OPTIMIZER

Privacy policy

Updated October 17, 2026 · Includes version 0.24.9
Developer: Michael Batkins

This policy covers the ALPA Image Optimizer Chrome extension. It explains how the extension handles information when you optimize images in an ALPA Higher Logic Real Magnet newsletter. The extension is independently developed and is not an official Higher Logic product. This page is hosted on Runway Rush for convenience; this policy covers the extension.

What the extension does

The extension remembers supported image-upload metadata and replaces eligible smaller image sources with recognized originals when you choose Optimize. It preserves display dimensions and surrounding newsletter content. It does not send email, choose recipients, or publish a newsletter.

Reviewed crop reconstruction (version 0.24.9)

For supported crops captured during your editing session, the extension downloads the recorded original and smaller image from Higher Logic, compares the native crop locally, and reconstructs a higher-resolution copy while preserving the crop, blank areas and displayed dimensions. You must review and approve the proposed crop. Applying it uploads the generated image file to the Higher Logic asset folder recorded for that crop, using your existing authenticated Higher Logic session, then updates the newsletter through its existing save controller. The original assets are not deleted. No image is sent to an AI service or developer collection server.

Crop instructions and export hashes are temporary session data. Optimize promptly after inserting images in the same editing session, before closing the newsletter, restarting the browser or updating the extension. Reliable optimization days later is not promised. The local upload inventory may retain bounded metadata for up to 30 days; that does not guarantee retention of crop instructions. Hosted generated images remain in your Higher Logic account until you manage or remove them there.

Information handled

  • Newsletter content: the extension reads newsletter HTML when you preview, optimize, or recover a change. It stores a local recovery copy before changing content. That copy can include email text, image references, links, names, addresses, and other information you placed in the draft.
  • Upload and resize activity: supported upload/resize requests and responses are inspected to extract filenames, file types and sizes, hosted image URLs, image dimensions, response field names, and limited operation diagnostics. Upload observation extracts metadata. Supported resize/crop capture also reads canvas export bytes locally to calculate hashes, and records draw coordinates, dimensions, image encoding and smoothing settings so an exact crop can be reconstructed. These bytes are not sent to the developer. Optional visual comparison can read hosted image files and pixels as described below.
  • Editor context: supported editor URLs, newsletter identifiers, frame-navigation events, session identifiers, timestamps, and bounded diagnostics are used to attach observation to the intended newsletter and identify matches.
  • Preferences and pairs: the extension stores your preferences and confirmed or observed image-pair metadata.

The extension does not collect passwords, authentication cookies, payment details, precise device location, or browsing activity from unrelated websites.

Optional local picture comparison (version 0.20 and later)

If you explicitly enable visual comparison and approve image-host access, the extension downloads eligible hosted newsletter images from maassets.higherlogic.com and compares their pixels locally in your browser. This helps match resized copies whose filenames differ from recorded originals. Candidate originals come from upload records associated with the selected newsletter; the extension does not search the entire image library.

Image files and derived comparison profiles are held in memory during the scan, not stored in the extension’s persistent image database. They are not sent to the developer, an AI service, or an analytics service. Requests reach Higher Logic’s image host, which may receive normal request information such as your IP address. The extension’s comparison downloads omit credentials and referrer information and refuse redirects. URL metadata and recovery copies can remain stored locally as described in this policy. You can disable comparison using its checkbox in the popup; this does not remove previously stored URL records or recovery copies.

Purpose and storage

This information is used for image matching, requested source replacement, recovery, and troubleshooting. Extension records are stored locally in Chrome extension storage, not in a separate developer database. The extension does not use Chrome storage sync to send these records to other devices.

Editor access is limited to alpa-rm.higherlogic.com and ww1.magnetmail.net, the Higher Logic wrapper, embedded editor, and asset manager. Optional access to maassets.higherlogic.com supports local picture comparison in version 0.20 and later. Navigation access follows editor frame changes. Clipboard access is write-only and is used when you explicitly choose Copy result in advanced tools; clipboard contents are not read.

Sharing and network behavior

The extension has no separate collection server or analytics service. It does not sell user data or use it for advertising. It uses Higher Logic's existing editor and save controller to save changes you request to your Higher Logic account. The browser may load replacement image URLs normally. Those existing service requests remain subject to Higher Logic's policies.

Reports and recovery files you download remain on your device. If you choose to send one for support, it may contain newsletter content, filenames, or URLs. Only send information you intend to share. Support correspondence is used to respond to your request.

Retention and control

Image-upload inventory is limited to 200 records. Records older than 30 days are excluded when inventory is processed or updated. Observation sessions have a 30-minute deadline; another supported navigation or opening the popup can start a new bounded session. Session diagnostics are held separately in temporary extension session storage.

Confirmed pairs, settings, and the latest recovery copy can persist locally until replaced or removed. Advanced tools let you disable automatic observation and manage observation records and saved pairs. Removing the extension clears its extension storage. Downloaded reports and recovery files remain until you delete them separately.

Limited use

Use of information by ALPA Image Optimizer complies with the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. Information is used only to provide or improve the extension's stated image-optimization and recovery features, not for advertising or unrelated profiling.
